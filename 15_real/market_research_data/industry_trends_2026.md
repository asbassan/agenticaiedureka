# CRM Industry Trends - 2026

Compiled from public market research and vendor blogs as of August 2026 (see
citations per section).

## Market Size and AI Adoption

The global CRM market is projected to reach approximately $126 billion in 2026,
growing at a compound annual rate above 12%. 91% of companies with 10+
employees now use CRM software, and 83% of companies report using AI features
within their CRM. Companies using AI in their CRM are 83% more likely to
exceed their sales goals than those that don't - but 55% of CRM
implementations still fail to meet their objectives, mostly due to data-entry
friction and poor user adoption, not the AI capability itself.
Source: [salesmate.io](https://www.salesmate.io/blog/crm-statistics/)

## AI Is Now a Pricing Battleground, Not Just a Feature

How vendors price AI has become a genuine competitive differentiator: HubSpot
bundles its Breeze AI across hubs at no additional per-user cost, while
Salesforce charges a per-user premium for Einstein and has moved parts of its
newer Agentforce pricing to a per-completed-task model - meaning the AI agent
itself is effectively billed like an additional "user." Zoho's Zia AI and
AI-agent features are gated to its Professional tier and above rather than
included at every level.
Source: [blog.salesflare.com](https://blog.salesflare.com/compare-salesforce-zoho-hubspot-pipedrive)

## Pricing Models Are Shifting Away from Pure Per-Seat

2026 SaaS pricing trends show a broader shift away from fixed per-seat pricing
toward usage-based, consumption-based, and outcome-based models - driven
largely by AI agents acting as autonomous "users" in their own right, which
per-seat pricing wasn't designed to account for. This increases cost
variability and complicates budgeting for buyers compared to predictable flat
per-seat pricing.
Source: [bigin.com](https://www.bigin.com/articles/seven-crm-trends-that-will-define-2026-and-2027.html)

## Buyer Priorities: Adoption Friction Over Feature Count

Buyers increasingly prioritize ease of adoption, transparent pricing
(including AI usage fees), integration with tools already in use, and clean
data exportability - over raw feature count. This favors vendors like
Pipedrive and HubSpot's lower tiers, which are explicitly positioned around
fast onboarding, over feature-maximalist platforms that require dedicated
administrators to configure.
Source: [salesmate.io](https://www.salesmate.io/blog/crm-statistics/),
[blog.salesflare.com](https://blog.salesflare.com/compare-salesforce-zoho-hubspot-pipedrive)
