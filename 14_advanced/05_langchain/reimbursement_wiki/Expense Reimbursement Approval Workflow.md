# Expense Reimbursement Approval Workflow (Internal Wiki)

## Step-by-step process
1. Employee submits an expense report through the Expense Management System (EMS) and attaches receipts.
2. EMS automatically routes the claim to the employee's direct manager for first-level approval.
3. If the manager approves and the total exceeds the Finance review threshold, EMS auto-escalates the claim to the Finance team's shared review queue.
4. Finance checks the claim against current reimbursement limits and confirms all receipts are attached correctly.
5. Once fully approved, the claim is marked "Ready for Payment" and picked up in the next payroll run.
6. The employee can track status anytime from the "My Expenses" dashboard; EMS sends an email notification at every stage change.

## Escalation
If a manager does not act on a claim within 5 business days, EMS automatically escalates it to that manager's own manager.

## Who to contact
- Questions about what qualifies for reimbursement: Finance Policy team (finance-policy@company.com)
- Technical issues submitting a claim in EMS: IT Helpdesk
